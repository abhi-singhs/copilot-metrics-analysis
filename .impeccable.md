## Design Context

### Users
This dashboard is used by enterprise and organization admins, engineering leaders, and analytics-minded stakeholders reviewing GitHub Copilot usage exports. They use it locally to inspect adoption, engagement, model usage, and code-assist effectiveness without sending metrics to a third-party service.

### Brand Personality
GitHub-native, analytical, pragmatic. The interface should feel trustworthy, crisp, and efficient so people can scan the data quickly and share findings with confidence.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a data-first visual language with strong hierarchy, restrained accents, and clear chart surfaces. The dashboard should feel like a polished analytical tool rather than a marketing page, leaning on GitHub-adjacent clarity and contrast while keeping the experience calm and local-first.

### Design Principles
1. Make the most important adoption signals obvious at a glance.
2. Favor clarity and comparison over decorative effects.
3. Keep controls close to the analysis so filtering feels fast and direct.
4. Treat dense data as readable by default with strong spacing, labels, and table ergonomics.
5. Reinforce trust through plain language, transparent local-only behavior, and consistent visual structure.
